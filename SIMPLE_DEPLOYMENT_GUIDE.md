## 🚀 **Deployment Steps:**

### **Step 1: Upload Files to Hostinger**
1. **Go to Hostinger File Manager**
2. **Navigate to `public_html`**
3. **Upload these files:**
   - `index.html` (main site)
   - `app.js` (functionality)
   - `.htaccess` (server config)
   - `Products/` folder (product images)

### **Step 2: Test Your Site**
Visit: `https://restntravel.shop`

## 🔧 **Customization Options:**

### **Add More Products:**
Edit `app.js` and add products to the `products` array.

### **Change Colors:**
Edit the CSS classes in `index.html` (uses Tailwind CSS).

### **Add Email Functionality:**
Replace the `sendContactEmail()` and `sendOrderEmail()` functions in `app.js`.

### **Add Payment Gateway:**
Replace the `checkout()` function with your preferred payment processor.
